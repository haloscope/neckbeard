# Gate 2 requires reading the relevant records, and records nothing about it

**Disposition (ADR-0007):** refinement decides

Gate 2 says to read the relevant decisions and reviews before proposing
options. At an adopting project documents were repeatedly opened and
skimmed rather than read, and the resulting proposals contradicted content
that was present in them.

This is the weakest item of the nine and is filed with the doubt attached
rather than hidden: it may be behaviour that no framework rule can reach. A
required list of "records read" would be trivially satisfied by listing
filenames, which would turn the framework's own evidence rule into theatre
- worse than no rule. If the refinement concludes there is no mechanism
worth having, closing this as rejected with that reasoning is a better
outcome than a ritual.

*Evidence: [second hand-back](../../docs/sources/feldtest-sustained-operation-2026-08.md), item 8.*
